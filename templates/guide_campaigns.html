Campaign flow

A campaign ties together a template (or A/B pair), a list of contacts, and a send action. jupsend queues emails, respects your Gmail rate limits, and records opens and clicks per contact on your dashboard.

Campaign types

  • One-time send — pick a template (optional A/B), add contacts, send once.
  • Automated follow-ups — attach a published workflow; launching starts the workflow for every contact on the campaign. Replies stop remaining steps.

Design workflows under Workflows, then attach them when creating a campaign.

Step-by-step (one-time send)

  1. 1Connect Gmail in Settings (required before any send).
  2. 2Create at least one template. Optionally add Template B for A/B testing.
  3. 3Create a campaign — pick Template A (and B if you want).
  4. 4On the campaign page, add contacts: select existing, paste rows, or upload Excel. Variable columns must match your template.
  5. 5Review personalized previews, then Send now or schedule for later.
  6. 6Watch the dashboard Contact activity feed and campaign analytics for opens, clicks, and bounces.

A/B assignment

Contacts alternate between Variant A and B (when Template B exists). Compare performance under campaign analytics after sending.
