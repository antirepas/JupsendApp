Campaign flow

A campaign ties together a template (or A/B pair), a list of contacts, and a send action. jupsend queues emails, respects your Gmail rate limits, and records opens and clicks per contact on your dashboard.

Campaign types

  • One-time send — pick a template (optional A/B), add contacts, send once.
  • Automated follow-ups — attach a published workflow; launching starts the workflow for every contact on the campaign.

Design workflows under Workflows, then attach them when creating a campaign.

Step-by-step (one-time send)

  1. 1Import contacts (paste or Excel) so you have recipients ready.
  2. 2Create at least one template. Optionally add Template B for A/B testing.
  3. 3Create a campaign — pick Template A (and B if you want).
  4. 4On the campaign page, add contacts from your list or import more. Variable columns must match your template.
  5. 5Review personalized previews, then Send now or schedule for later.
  6. 6Watch the dashboard Contact activity feed and campaign analytics for opens and clicks.

Run experiments (A/B)

When you add Template B, label what you're testing (subject, opener, offer) and write a short hypothesis. jupsend picks a winner by open rate once enough sends land, compares the campaign to your 30-day average, and lets you promote the winner as a new template.

Your outreach lab

The dashboard tracks your open-rate benchmark, personal best, and recent experiments. Use Interested contacts to see who clicked or opened — then follow up.

A/B assignment

Contacts alternate between Variant A and B (when Template B exists). Compare performance under campaign analytics after sending.
