Campaign folders

A folder is the workspace: templates + contacts sheet + Manage. Creating the folder creates the draft campaign. Files you add/remove sync into Manage while the campaign is still editable (not sent / not scheduled).

Campaign types (on Manage)

  • One-time send — single template blast.
  • One-time A/B — needs two templates in the folder; alternates A/B on the first email.
  • Sequence — email → wait → follow-ups configured in Edit sequence.
  • Sequence + A/B first email — A/B on touch one, then shared follow-ups.

Legacy “workflow mode” campaigns should be switched to Sequence on Manage before launch.

Scheduling

  • Absolute time — one datetime for everyone (your timezone).
  • Local time — pick a location column, a date, and a clock time. Each contact gets that local hour (e.g. 9:00 in New York and 9:00 in Greece).

Launch checklist

  1. Mailbox send-ready; enough seats for the volume you plan.
  2. Folder has template(s) and a contacts sheet with mapped variables.
  3. Type + sequence steps set; temperature / stop / reply watchers as needed.
  4. Preview a few contacts → send now or schedule.
  5. Watch queue progress, then Interested + analytics.

Common mistakes

  • Deleting the contacts sheet and wondering why Manage still showed people (fixed on sync — keep the sheet).
  • Sequence follow-ups with no template assigned.
  • Local-time schedule with empty/unresolvable location values.
