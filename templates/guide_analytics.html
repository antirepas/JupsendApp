What to optimize for

Perfect outreach optimizes for replies (and meetings), not vanity opens. Opens help diagnose; clicks show curiosity; replies are the goal.

Where to look

  • Dashboard — account pulse, activity feed, goals after first sends.
  • Campaign analytics — variant performance, funnel, per-contact engagement.
  • Workflow analytics — where contacts sit in the graph; which steps leak.

How to iterate

  1. If open rate is fine but replies are low — rewrite ask/CTA, not just subject.
  2. If clicks are high — warm path is working; tighten hot email.
  3. If cold path never converts — shorten sequence or improve targeting.
  4. Use A/B for one change at a time; promote winners.
