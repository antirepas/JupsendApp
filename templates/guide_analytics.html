Optimize for replies

Positive replies (and meetings) are the north star. Opens and clicks are optional diagnostics when you enable tracking — not the goal.

Where to look

  • Dashboard — recent activity, plan usage, goals after you start sending.
  • Campaign Manage / analytics — reply mix, engagement, A/B performance.
  • Mailbox deliverability — DNS health, bounces, seat reputation.
  • Billing — monthly email and contact usage vs plan caps.

How to iterate

  1. Low replies → fix targeting and ask before vanity subjects.
  2. A/B one change at a time; winners lean on reply rate.
  3. Turn on click tracking only when you need that signal for temperature or follow-ups.
  4. Stop or pause campaigns that burn the domain — reputation > volume.
