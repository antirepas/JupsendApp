What to optimize for

Perfect outreach optimizes for positive replies (and meetings). Opens and clicks are optional diagnostics when you enable tracking on a step.

Where to look

  • Dashboard — reply rates, activity feed, goals after first sends.
  • Campaign analytics — positive vs negative replies, funnel, per-contact engagement.
  • Workflow analytics — where contacts sit in the graph; which steps leak.
  • Mailbox deliverability — DNS auth, Mail-Tester, bounce/reply health.

How to iterate

  1. If replies are low — rewrite ask/CTA and targeting, not vanity subject lines.
  2. Label negative replies so they stop sequences; promote positive ones from Interested.
  3. Enable click tracking only on later follow-ups when you need that signal.
  4. Use A/B for one change at a time; winners are chosen by reply rate.
