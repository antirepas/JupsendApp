What workflows do

Workflows define multi-step automation: send, wait, branch on opens/clicks/replies, then follow up. You build them in the visual editor, publish, and attach them to a campaign — contacts only enter a workflow when you launch that campaign.

Building a workflow

  1. Create templates for each email step.
  2. Go to Workflows → New and open the visual builder.
  3. Drag nodes: Send email, Wait, Condition (opened? clicked? replied?), End.
  4. Connect nodes with edges; set template and delay on each action node.
  5. Publish the workflow, then create a campaign and choose Automated follow-ups to attach it.
  6. Add contacts to the campaign and click Start workflow.

Campaigns vs workflows

  • Campaign — where you add contacts and launch (one-time send or workflow)
  • Workflow — the automation blueprint (send/wait/branch steps)
  • Individual send — one email to one contact from the Sends page
