Variable syntax guide

Basics

Simple personalization from contact data.

{{"{{name}}"}}, {{"{{company}}"}}

Mailbox (sender)

Filled from whichever mailbox sends — use for signatures when seats rotate. Not a contact field.

Best regards, {{"{{@name}}"}} {{"{{@first_name}}"}}, {{"{{@email}}"}}

Formatting

First name, title case, and fallback when empty.

{{"{{name|first|default:there}}"}} {{"{{company|title}}"}}

Required & fallback

Missing required values skip that contact at send.

{{"{{company|required}}"}} {{"{{role|default:your team}}"}}

Optional blocks

Hide a paragraph when the variable is empty.

{% if description %}I noticed {{"{{description}}"}}.{% endif %}

Links

Validate and wrap URLs as clickable links in the body.

{{"{{landing|url}}"}}

Truncate

Limit text to a word count (deterministic).

{{"{{bio|truncate:30}}"}}
{{if .aiEnabled}}

AI (uses credits at send)

Fit text into a sentence grammatically, or summarize to N words.

{{"{{~description}}"}} {{"{{bio|summarize:30}}"}}
{{else}}

AI filters

AI not configured — {{"{{~var}}"}} and {{"{{var|summarize:N}}"}} require OpenAI.

{{end}}
