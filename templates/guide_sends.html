What Sends shows

Every outbound email becomes a send record in a background queue: pending → sent (or failed), then opens, clicks, and replies when tracking is enabled. Use Sends (and Inbox → Sent) to verify delivery before you trust campaign totals.

How to read a send

  • Delivery status — pending/queued, sent, failed. Failures usually mean mailbox/SMTP issues.
  • Scheduled time — absolute or per-contact local time may leave jobs waiting until their slot.
  • Human opens — bots/scanners are filtered; don’t panic over instant “opens.”
  • Clicks — stronger intent when tracking is on.

Habits

  1. After launch, spot-check a handful of sends for “sent.”
  2. If the queue stalls, check Mailboxes limits (monthly plan cap, spacing, seat status).
  3. Use send detail when a contact says they never got it.
