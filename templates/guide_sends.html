What Sends shows

Every outbound email becomes a send record: queued → sent (or failed), then opens, clicks, replies. Use this page to verify delivery before you trust campaign stats.

How to read a send

  • Delivery status — queued, sent, failed. Failed needs SMTP/mailbox attention.
  • Human opens — bots/scanners are filtered; don’t panic over instant “opens.”
  • Clicks — strong intent signal for warm/hot rules.
  • Preview — in-app HTML is sanitized so tracking pixels/links don’t fire false events.

Perfect-outreach habits

  1. After launch, spot-check 5–10 sends for “sent.”
  2. If opens look weird, check bot/scanned metrics on the send detail.
  3. Use send detail when a contact says “never got it.”
