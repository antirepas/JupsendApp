What Google does

Sign in with Google creates your jupsend account and session. It is not the same as “send through my Gmail” for Free/Pro outreach seats.

Where sending comes from

  • Free — shared SMTP mailbox managed by the app.
  • Pro — your InboxKit domain seats (or attached SMTP).

See Mailboxes playbook for setup.
