Google for accounts only

jupsend uses Google sign-in to create and log into your account (openid, email, profile). It does not request Gmail send access.

Outbound email uses your Free shared mailbox or Pro InboxKit mailboxes — manage those under Mailboxes.

Step-by-step

  1. 1Choose Free or Pro on the pricing page, then continue with Google.
  2. 2Sign in with Google and allow basic profile/email access.
  3. 3Set up sending on Mailboxes (shared SMTP on Free, domain + mailboxes on Pro).
  4. 4Optional: Test SMTP from server to confirm outbound mail works from your host.
