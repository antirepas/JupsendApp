Why connect Gmail?

jupsend connects your Gmail with Google OAuth (no shared SMTP password). Credentials are OAuth tokens; delivery uses SMTP XOAUTH2 to smtp.gmail.com. Opens and clicks are tracked on your jupsend domain.

  • Send campaigns and one-off emails from your address
  • Daily send limits protect your sender reputation

Step-by-step

  1. 1Go to Settings and find the Gmail section.
  2. 2Click Connect Gmail. You'll be redirected to Google to sign in and approve access.
  3. 3Grant permission to send email on your behalf (jupsend does not read your inbox).
  4. 4After redirect back, click Test SMTP from server to confirm ports 465/587 work from your host.
  5. 5Set your From name if needed, then click Save settings.

Tips

Your VPS must allow outbound SMTP (465/587). If the SMTP test times out or says ports are blocked, fix the host network before sending campaigns.

To revoke access, remove jupsend from your Google Account permissions at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
