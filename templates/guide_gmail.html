Why connect Gmail?

jupsend sends outreach through your Gmail account (not a shared SMTP password). Opens and clicks are tracked via your jupsend domain, while delivery uses Google's servers — better deliverability and fewer auth issues.

  • Send campaigns and one-off emails from your address
  • Automatic bounce detection via IMAP (failed addresses get suppressed)
  • Rate limits and warmup to protect your sender reputation

Step-by-step

  1. 1Go to Settings and find the Gmail section.
  2. 2Click Connect Gmail. You'll be redirected to Google to sign in and approve access.
  3. 3Grant permission for Gmail send + read (used only for bounces, not browsing your inbox).
  4. 4After redirect back, Settings will show your connected email. Set your From name and adjust send limits if needed, then click Save settings.

Tips

Start with low daily limits (warmup is on by default) and increase gradually. If Google asks for app verification in production, test users can connect during development.

To disconnect, use Disconnect Gmail in Settings — tokens are removed from jupsend immediately.
