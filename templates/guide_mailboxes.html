Why mailboxes come first

Recipients and filters judge the sender. Perfect outreach protects reputation: right mailbox type, sane volume, warm-up on new domains.

Free vs Pro

  • Free — shared sending address configured by the app. Good to learn the product; limited volume.
  • Pro — buy a domain/mailboxes via InboxKit, or add a domain you own (DNS) / external SMTP. Wait until status is ready, then send.

Google sign-in is only for logging into jupsend — not for sending as Gmail OAuth by default.

Perfect-outreach setup

  1. Use a dedicated outreach domain (not your main website domain if you can avoid it).
  2. Real first/last names on seats — From name builds trust.
  3. Start small daily volume; enable warmup where available — limits rise when you actually send, not just by waiting.
  4. Test SMTP from Mailboxes after provisioning.

Common mistakes

  • Pointing nameservers on a domain that still hosts a live site’s DNS.
  • Blasting thousands on day one of a new domain.
  • Ignoring seats stuck in provisioning — stay on the status page / Mailboxes sync.
