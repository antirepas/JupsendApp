Why mailboxes matter

Recipients and filters judge the sender. Plans (Starter → Hyper) set monthly email and contact caps; domains and seats are add-ons you buy or connect under Mailboxes — after you’ve explored the rest of the app.

When to set this up

  • You can create folders, contacts, templates, and sequences with no mailbox yet.
  • When you’re ready to send: buy InboxKit domains/seats, import a domain you own, or attach SMTP credentials.
  • Match seat count to plan volume — one cold mailbox won’t safely carry a Hyper month alone.

Google sign-in is only for logging into jupsend — not your outreach From address by default.

Setup checklist

  1. Prefer a dedicated outreach domain (not the one hosting your marketing site, if you can avoid it).
  2. Real first/last names on seats — From name builds trust.
  3. Keep spacing and per-minute limits conservative while the domain warms.
  4. Send a test from Mailboxes after a seat shows ready.
  5. Match plan volume to seat count — one cold mailbox cannot safely absorb a Hyper month alone.

Common mistakes

  • Buying Hyper (or Scale) without enough seats for the monthly volume.
  • Pointing nameservers on a domain that still hosts a live site’s DNS.
  • Blasting thousands on day one of a new domain.
  • Ignoring seats stuck in provisioning — stay on Mailboxes until status is ready.
