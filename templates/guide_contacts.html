Contacts live with the campaign

In a campaign folder, add a contacts sheet. jupsend snapshots that sheet onto the campaign for sending. Deleting the sheet from the folder clears the campaign audience on the next sync.

Import & variables

  1. Open the sheet in the folder (or Contacts → Import for global lists).
  2. Map columns to variables your templates use (first_name, company, country, …).
  3. For local-time sends, include a location column (country, city, or IANA timezone).
  4. Keep one best email per row; suppress bounces and unsubscribes.

Plan limits

Paid plans cap how many contacts you can store (e.g. Starter 5K → Hyper 25K). Existing contacts still count; upgrades raise the ceiling.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving first_name unmapped → empty greetings.
  • Expecting Manage to keep contacts after deleting the folder sheet.
  • Scheduling local-time sends without a resolvable location column.
