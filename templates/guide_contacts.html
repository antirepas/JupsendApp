Why list quality wins

Deliverability and reply rate start with who you email. Perfect outreach uses a focused list with real personalization fields — not a scraped megadump.

Exact steps

  1. Open Contacts → Import. Paste or upload Excel/CSV.
  2. Map columns to variables your templates use (first_name, company, etc.).
  3. If a cell has multiple emails, jupsend prefers hello@ → contact@ → support@ → first valid.
  4. Put people on a named list so campaigns stay organized.
  5. Suppress bounces / unsubscribes so you don’t re-mail them.

Perfect-outreach recipe

  • One ICP per campaign (same role / industry).
  • Verify you can fill every required template variable.
  • Remove role accounts (info@, sales@) unless that’s the ICP.
  • Refresh lists before relaunch — don’t keep dead emails.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving first_name unmapped → empty greetings.
  • Importing the same people into every campaign without suppressions.
  • Skipping sample download — column mapping errors are hard to spot later.
