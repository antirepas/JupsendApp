What is a template?

A template is the email your contacts receive: subject line + HTML body. Use placeholders like {{"{{name}}"}} or {{"{{company}}"}} — jupsend replaces them with each contact's data when sending.

Creating a template

  1. Open Templates → New template.
  2. Enter an internal name and subject line.
  3. Write the body in the visual editor — use the toolbar for bold, lists, and links.
  4. Add personalization with {{"{{name}}"}} placeholders in the subject or body. Variables are detected automatically and shown as chips.
  5. Watch the live preview on the right; edit sample values to see how placeholders render.
  6. Links in the body are rewritten for click tracking when the email is sent.

Example subject: Quick question for {{"{{name}}"}} at {{"{{company}}"}}

Optional AI assists

When OPENAI_API_KEY is set, the template editor offers subtle suggestions: rewrite short selections, subject line ideas, lint-style hints, a tone check before save, and structural starters for empty drafts. Nothing is changed unless you click to apply it.

Best practices

  • Keep one clear call-to-action link per email
  • Match variables to columns when importing contacts (paste or Excel)
  • Use Template A / B in campaigns to A/B test two versions
