Why templates

Templates are your scalable voice. Perfect outreach templates sound like one thoughtful email — with placeholders that only insert facts you actually have.

Exact steps

  1. Create a template: name, subject, body.
  2. Use {{"{{variable}}"}} for personalization. Lint/preview before saving.
  3. Optional AI assists (rewrite, subjects, tone) — always edit for truth.
  4. On the editor, open the variable syntax guide for filters and defaults.

Perfect-outreach recipe

  • Subject: specific, no clickbait; 4–7 words often win.
  • Body: 50–125 words, one problem, one ask, easy reply (“Worth a 15‑min chat?”).
  • Sign with {{"{{@name}}"}} so the closing matches the mailbox that sent (rotating seats).
  • CTA link only if it helps — tracked clicks feed warm/hot rules.
  • Write a separate warm follow-up template — don’t reuse the intro verbatim.

Common mistakes

  • Variables that don’t exist on contacts.
  • Walls of text or three CTAs.
  • Same template for hot and cold branches.
