Why this page exists

Automation finds interest; humans close it. Interested contacts are people with positive (or non-negative) replies — sorted so you work the hottest first. Opens and clicks no longer fill this queue.

Perfect follow-up habit

  1. Check Interested daily (or after each campaign wave).
  2. Prioritize positive replies; correct AI labels when needed on the conversation.
  3. Reply in-thread from your real mailbox conversation when possible.
  4. Dismiss noise so the list stays actionable.

The Loom + Calendly close

In the recommended playbook, contacts who stay hot or warm through the sequence hit an End node on purpose — automation stops so you can close personally:

  1. Record a short Loom tailored to their company / thread (60–90 seconds).
  2. Send it from your real inbox with your Calendly (or booking) link.
  3. Don’t put another generic template after that End — that’s the handoff from system to human.

Common mistakes

  • Letting automation keep “following up” while you ignore a reply (sequence stops, but silence from you kills deals).
  • Treating bot opens as interest — cross-check send detail.
  • Skipping the personal Loom for hot/warm leads and sending another blast instead.
