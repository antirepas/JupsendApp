Why this queue exists

Sequences find interest; humans close it. Interested surfaces contacts with positive (or non-negative) replies so you work the hottest first. Opens and clicks alone don’t fill this list.

Daily habit

  1. Check Interested after each send wave (or daily).
  2. Prioritize positive replies; correct labels on the conversation when the model is wrong.
  3. Reply in-thread from Inbox when you can.
  4. Dismiss noise so the list stays actionable.

Closing warm leads

When someone engages, stop relying on the next automated bump. A short personal note (or Loom) plus a booking link beats another template. Sequences can stop on reply — silence from you still kills deals.

Common mistakes

  • Ignoring Interested while the campaign keeps running.
  • Treating bot opens as interest — check send detail.
  • Sending another blast instead of a personal close for hot leads.
