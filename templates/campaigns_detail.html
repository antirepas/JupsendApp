How this workflow runs

When you launch this campaign, each contact gets their own copy of the workflow below. Steps run automatically — you don't need to do anything per contact.

  • Send — an email goes out from your connected Gmail account
  • Wait — the contact pauses until the delay finishes
  • Branch — paths can split in parallel; each branch is evaluated by priority
  • Replies stop the sequence — remaining steps are cancelled for that contact
